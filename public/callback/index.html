Before you sign up

Questions doctors ask us

What happens on the call?

A short conversation with the founder about how your practice runs, then we get you set up to try EazeNote. There is nothing to pay on the call.

Who is EazeNote for?

Independent GPs, psychologists in private practice and other independent clinicians who write their own notes, letters and codes.

Does it make clinical decisions?

No. EazeNote writes up the consult for you to check. It does not diagnose, and nothing is saved until you sign.

My patients switch languages. Does that work?

EazeNote is built for the way South Africans actually speak, including consults that move between languages.

What does it cost?

R3 per signed note, bought as prepaid credits. There is no monthly subscription and no lock-in. The first 20 practices start free.

Do I need to change my practice software?

No. You copy or export the signed note into the system you already use.
